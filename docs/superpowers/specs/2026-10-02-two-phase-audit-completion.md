# Two phase production audit completion

Binding sources are the September 29 repair plan, Revised Design v2, and the decisions in `docs/two-phase-production-audit.md`. The October 2 audit found that compiler success and the existing fourteen database scenarios do not establish completion.

## Required repairs and acceptance

1. Material costs stored to two decimal places must be the amounts summed into mixing and packaging totals. Fractional ingredient and packaging deductions must reconcile with recovery and finished stock value. A database regression must cover two deductions costing 0.005 each.
2. Formula, mixing and operator lists must expose records beyond the default first ten. Search and page controls must operate on the entire matching result set. Creation selectors must expose all active formulas rather than silently truncating at one hundred; use lightweight option rows. Assigned-reader constraints must remain server enforced.
3. Creation and definition mutations must invalidate the operator lists and embedded manufacturing data they change. Hook tests must exercise successful callbacks against a real QueryClient.
4. Console actions must match server permissions. Missing/denied allocations must show an error or empty state. Managers must have accessible cancellation actions for untouched work; formula management must expose deactivation and editable instructions, notes and minimum yield.
5. Bulk remaining must use decimal arithmetic, including 0.300 minus 0.100. Allocation entry must preview containers, full cartons and loose units using the same unit conversion as server validation, through the existing `getPackagingTarget` helper.
6. Integrity checks must inspect both carton-only and ledger-only allocation groups. They must also reconcile finished stock quantity with carton contents and inventory value with quantity times WAC, accounting for recipe carton capacity. A scoped check must not inspect unrelated allocations. Database checks must reproduce missing groups and finished stock drift.
7. Close remaining evidence gaps with clean migrations, relation queries, report/dashboard queries, and independent-connection dispatch versus bulk/return races. Re-run unit tests, typecheck and build after repairs.
8. Allocation carton management and integrity results must be reachable from the packaging list. The removed legacy route left its management component unused; reuse that component with an explicit allocation ID through a current allocation cartons route. Stock-only failures must remain visible with recipe, warehouse, quantity and valuation details.
9. Existing invoice edit/delete handlers and the inventory stock-transfer endpoint must reconcile allocation cartons, finished stock, value and ledger records just as invoice creation and carton transfers do. Inventory availability and invoice form previews must not count partial carton contents twice. Invoice product selection must include sealed standard cartons, eligible partial packs for unit lines and loose-only recipes, while excluding held stock and respecting other lines' reservations. Reproduce invoice create/edit/delete and full/partial/loose stock transfers through these actual reachable handlers before repairing them; retain transactional locking and preserve sales/finance records.
10. Warehouse deactivation transfers must preserve carton location, stock quantity and valuation, and must not remove stock needed by live manufacturing work. Acquire every source/destination finished-stock lock in one global warehouse/recipe order before relocating multiple recipes, matching bulk carton mutations. Raw stock additions and adjustments must serialize with manufacturing debits so an unlocked absolute update cannot overwrite consumption. Inventory readers must retain canonical stock quantities; physical carton counts remain separate, and partial packs contribute once to displayed units, stock status and value.
11. Sealed cartons must remain available consistently to invoice creation and editing. An explicitly recorded zero WAC is a valid cost and must remain zero through invoice create/edit/delete and approved returns. Invoice profit displays must use recorded COGS even when it is zero, rather than a rounded margin estimate. Warehouse deactivation locks must allow stock-audit foreign-key checks without deadlocking against stock mutations while still excluding new manufacturing admission. Full and partial warehouse/carton transfers must credit exactly the inventory value debited, including the final rounding residual from four-decimal WAC. Online invoice COGS must use that recorded debit, and editing/deleting the invoice must restore the recorded value; signed offline historical COGS remains immutable. Full or repeated approved sales returns must conserve recorded invoice COGS, taking the remaining currency rounding amount on the final return. A further packaging receipt must add its recorded production cost to existing recorded inventory value before deriving WAC, retaining the existing rounding remainder.

## Constraints and rulings

- Preserve all existing uncommitted and unrelated work. Do not commit, reset or directly edit the generated route tree.
- Legacy data migration/display is out of scope, per the existing owner's testing decision.
- Retain completion-time finished goods receipt and existing immutable live definition guards.
- Ruling: use code tracing, component/hook checks and PostgreSQL lifecycle checks for screen verification. The owner explicitly prohibits Playwright and similar browser automation. No automated browser evidence is required for this pass; do not claim visual/manual browser verification.
- Use only isolated databases with names ending `_test`. Do not apply migrations or stock mutations to the application database.
- Track results in `docs/two-phase-production-audit.md`. Check findings only with passing evidence. Run `graphify update .` after code changes.
