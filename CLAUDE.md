## Product notes

- **Order booker self-service portal is not in production use.** The pages under the order booker portal (My Commission, orders, recoveries, trips, and its home page) exist in the code but no order booker uses them. Do not treat them as user-facing when planning features or specs: don't design for them, don't count them as impact, and don't spend effort updating them unless explicitly asked. Order booker data is viewed by managers through Sales → People → Order bookers and through HR payroll.

## Migrations

- **NEVER manually modify or tweak migration files** (SQL, snapshots, `meta/_journal.json` under `src/db/migrations/`). They are always generated by `bun run db:generate`. To change one, fix the schema source and generate a new forward migration.
